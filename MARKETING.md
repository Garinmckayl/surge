# Surge launch kit

## Positioning

**The world's opportunity stream, ranked for your startup.**

Surge is a founder-fit decision engine—not another directory. Directories show what exists. Surge helps a founder decide what is worth pursuing next.

## Product promise

Turn a founder profile into a source-backed action queue. Surge discovers grants, accelerators, and hackathons, then aligns each candidate to the startup's project, geography, eligibility, deadlines, and application capacity. Every rank comes with the source and the reason.

## 30-second pitch

Founders don't have a shortage of opportunities. They have a shortage of time to figure out which ones fit. Surge turns the global grants, accelerators, and hackathons landscape into a founder-specific decision queue. Jev supplies structured eligibility, project-fit, and effort judgments; Surge adds verified sources, deadline math, and the next action. Not another list—an engine for deciding what to do next.

## Launch post

**A directory tells you what exists. It doesn't tell you what you should do next.**

We're building **Surge**: the founder-fit decision engine for grants, accelerators, and hackathons.

Tell Surge what you're building, where you're based, your stage, and how much time you can spend applying. It searches live sources, checks deadlines, and ranks opportunities against your actual constraints—with a source and a reason behind every score.

The important part isn't a chatbot writing another paragraph. It's structured decisions that can be applied consistently across a much larger candidate pool: fit, eligibility, effort, deadline, next move.

Surge is built on AWS and powered by Jev's structured scoring under the hood. **The product is Surge: less browsing, better bets.**

Try the live build at https://surge.arcumet.com

## Positioning discipline

Lead with Surge and the founder outcome. Describe Jev as the scoring engine, not the product. Say “batch-ranked” when explaining the API. Avoid claiming complete worldwide coverage or a measured “thousands in seconds” result until the source catalog is broader and a unique-record throughput benchmark is published.

## What the screen proves

The dashboard turns each live scan into a visible market signal: category mix, founder-fit tiers, and deadline runway update with the ranked set. Then the shortlist itself moves into fit order, with score composition and official-source evidence on each opportunity. Surge makes the decision process legible—not just the answer.
